# Meetings

FEMU is starting two regular meetings, open to anyone who uses or changes FEMU.
Dates and the meeting link are announced on the
[mailing list](https://groups.google.com/g/femu) and on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/AgPTUJCw7).

| Meeting | How often | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community call | Monthly | What changed in FEMU, what is planned, and the features and fixes users ask for |
| Office hour | Monthly | Help with your own setup, experiment or patch, one question at a time |

:::info[Schedule]

The first dates are not set yet. Join the mailing list to get the invitation.

:::

## Propose a topic

Open a thread in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/MoatLab/FEMU/discussions) and say what
you want to discuss and why. Topics that come with a concrete case, such as a
failing command, a workload or a draft patch, go first.

## Notes

Notes from each call are posted in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/MoatLab/FEMU/discussions), so people in
other time zones can follow and reply.

## In person

The team meets users at storage and systems conferences. The most recent was
SNIA SDC 2026. If you will be at a venue and want to talk about FEMU, say so
on the mailing list.
