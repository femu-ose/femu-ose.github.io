FEMU has received a Phase I award from the U.S. National Science Foundation's
Pathways to Enable Open-Source Ecosystems (POSE) program:
[Award No. 2550145](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award/?AWD_ID=2550145),
*Toward a Community-Driven Fast Emulator (FEMU) Ecosystem for Next-Generation
Storage Systems Research and Innovation*, running from June 1, 2026 to May 31,
2027.

{/* truncate */}

POSE does not fund new emulator features. It funds the work that turns a
widely used research tool into a project its users can sustain together. The
award has three objectives:

1. **Ecosystem discovery and community engagement.** Learn who uses FEMU, for
   what, and what keeps people from contributing. We are interviewing users in
   universities and industry this fall.
2. **Contributor infrastructure and training.** Onboarding paths, continuous
   integration and testing, and teaching material. The
   [FEMU Manual](/manual) and its [nine tutorials](/manual/tutorials) are the
   first results.
3. **Governance and security.** A transparent governance charter and security
   policies. The [proposed charter](/docs/community/governance#proposed-charter-draft-for-comment)
   is open for comment, and the [security policy](https://github.com/MoatLab/FEMU/blob/master/SECURITY.md)
   is in the repository.

## How to take part

- **Tell us how you use FEMU.** Post in
  [Show and tell](https://github.com/MoatLab/FEMU/discussions/categories/show-and-tell):
  your lab, course or company, and what you emulate. It shapes what we build
  next.
- **Ask and discuss** on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AgPTUJCw7), in
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/MoatLab/FEMU/discussions), or on the
  [mailing list](https://groups.google.com/g/femu), femu@googlegroups.com.
- **Contribute** with the [first model test](/docs/community/first-test), a
  standalone exercise that needs no guest or KVM.
- **Teach with FEMU** using the [teaching guide](/docs/community/teaching).
